# Lineup Auction

1. Right-click "Start Lineup Auction.command" and choose Open, then Open again. (Only the first time. After that, just double-click it.)
2. If your Mac asks whether to allow incoming connections, click Allow.
3. The board opens in your browser with a QR code. Mates on the same wifi scan it and type their name. First one in is the host.

Close the Terminal window to stop the game.

If phones can't connect, your wifi is blocking devices from talking to each other. Turn on your phone's hotspot and put the laptop and every phone on it.
